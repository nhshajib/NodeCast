Use the TV edition on Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV, including Hisense Fire TV devices. It has a dedicated D-pad layout with an icon rail and visible focus. Use the verified store listing when available, or the official TV APK. On Fire TV, enable installation for Downloader only and open the official TV download page. The phone APK is not designed for your remote. Physical-device playback and remote behavior should be checked on your own model. Apple TV is not supported yet.

GlowTV is a player only. Add your own legally obtained M3U link, playlist file or Xtream account. No channels, subscriptions or content are provided.
